# Product Guidelines: Keystone

## Brand Identity
- **Personality:** Professional, reliable, and modern. Keystone should feel like a secure, high-tech platform for managing important life decisions (housing).
- **Tone:** Semi-technical and precise. Use industry-standard terminology (e.g., "Lease Agreement," "Compliance Document," "Offer Status") to provide clarity and instill confidence in our users.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Visual Clarity & Focus:** Prioritize white space, high-contrast typography, and clear visual hierarchies. The UI should guide the user through complex workflows without overwhelming them.
- **Mobile-First Responsiveness:** Every feature must be fully functional and aesthetically pleasing on mobile devices, as many users (especially tenants and agents) will be using the platform on the go.
- **Efficient Workflows:** Minimize unnecessary steps for frequent tasks. While clarity is key, we should also strive for speed and ease of use.

## Design System & Components
- **Framework:** Svelte with TailwindCSS 4.0.
- **Interactivity:** Use smooth, purposeful animations and transitions to provide feedback and maintain context during navigation.
- **Form Design:** Use clear labels, helpful error messages, and logical grouping of related fields.

## Security & Trust
- **Explicit Reassurance:** For sensitive actions like document uploads and identity verification, use clear security badges, reassuring copy (e.g., "Your documents are encrypted and stored securely"), and progress indicators.
- **Data Privacy:** Be transparent about how user data is used and stored.
